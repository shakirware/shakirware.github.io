Hi, I'm Shakir, a software engineer based in London.

## outside of work

I run two systems of my own:

- **[PharmacyShifts](/projects/pharmacyshifts/)** is a shift discovery platform I built from scratch and still operate, aggregating locum pharmacy shifts from three agencies.
- **[My homelab](/projects/homelab/)** is a self-hosted private cloud running around forty containers across six NixOS VMs on a single Proxmox server. I manage the infrastructure declaratively, with everything from networking and storage to monitoring defined in Git.

I also train and have competed in streetlifting, a strength sport involving weighted pull-ups, dips, muscle-ups and squats. It's also inspired two of my training tools.
